Introduction

A secure environment is built through clear architecture, consistent operations and continuous improvement.

Build a strong foundation

Start by understanding the assets you need to protect, the users and systems that need access, and the risks that matter most to your organization. Establish clear ownership for each control and document the expected behavior of the environment.

Apply layered safeguards

Use strong identity verification, least-privilege access, sensible network segmentation, secure configuration baselines and centralized monitoring. Review the controls regularly and test whether they work as intended.

  • Maintain an accurate inventory of users, devices, applications and network assets.
  • Apply access controls based on business need and verified identity.
  • Monitor important events and investigate unusual activity promptly.
  • Document changes, owners and recovery procedures.

Measure, review and improve

Make improvement part of the operating model. Measure meaningful outcomes, learn from incidents and changes, and keep people, processes and technology aligned as your environment evolves.

Every organization has a different risk profile. Adapt these recommendations to your environment, validate changes in a controlled way and keep operational documentation current.
